01 / The Product

Remember → Track → Reflect

01 / Discover

A vocabulary for practice

Browse 100+ tricks by level, then mark each one as learning, landed, or mastered—the smallest useful memory of where practice stands.

02 / Log

Log the session, not the admin

A fast flow captures the tricks, duration, notes, and feeling of a session without turning training into paperwork.

03 / Progress

See momentum at a glance

Level progress and training history make improvement visible between the sessions where it is hardest to notice.

04 / Personalize

A profile shaped by practice

A lightweight pole persona turns training patterns into a personal view of strengths, preferences, and what to explore next.

05 / Celebrate

Make progress visible

Milestones turn scattered practice into a record dancers can return to, recognize, and feel proud of.

06 / Capture

Add what the library misses

Custom tricks keep the diary useful across studios, naming systems, and the moves dancers create for themselves.

07 / Reflect

Your whole journey, together

Practice records, progress, favorite moves, and achievements resolve into one private place that belongs to the dancer.

02 / The Thinking

Pole dancers did not need another tutorial library. They needed a personal memory for their journey.

The product decisions followed that principle.

01

Simple progress states

Learning, landed, and mastered are clear enough to use in seconds, but meaningful enough to show movement over time.

02

Connected practice history

Tricks and sessions reinforce each other, so a dancer can move from “what am I working on?” to “how has it evolved?” without maintaining two separate systems.

03

Private by design

No account is required. Training data stays local, with backup and restore controlled by the person who created it.

Designed and built independently with React Native, Expo, TypeScript, and StoreKit—from first idea to launch.
