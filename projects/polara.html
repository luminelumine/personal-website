04 / About & Methodology

About

Polara is a private iPhone training journal for pole dancers. It helps dancers remember tricks, log sessions, and see progress over time; it is not a technique course or a replacement for an instructor.

Methodology

Tricks are organized as visual references with names, short descriptions, levels, and personal states. The states are intentionally simple—To learn, Learning, and Landed—so each dancer can record progress in their own practice context.

Author

Polara is independently designed, built, and maintained by Lumine, a product designer, developer, and pole dancer. First-hand practice shapes what the app records and what it deliberately leaves to qualified teaching.
